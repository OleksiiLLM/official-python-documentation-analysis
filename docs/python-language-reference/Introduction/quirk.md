# Quirk

In the contet of Python, **Quirks** refer to the unexpected, counter-intuitive, or highly specific behaviour of the language that often surprise developers becouse they seem to defy standard logic or behave differently than in other programming languages.

Think of it like a friend who has a very specific, slighlty weird habit - like eating pizza with a spoon. It is not necessarily "wrong" or a disease, but it is a unique eccentricity you have to memorize and account for when you interact with them.

In Python, a quirk is not a bug; it is a feature functioning exactly as it was designed, even if that design causes a few headaches for beginners.

Ultimately, knowing Python's quirks is a rite of passage. They are the edge cases and "gotchas" that you slowly memorize as you transiontion from a beginner to an experienced Python developer.
