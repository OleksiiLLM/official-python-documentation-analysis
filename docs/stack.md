# Stack

In the context of Python (and computer science in general), a **Stack** most commonly refers to a specific way of organizing data or tasks where the last item you put in is the very first item you take out. This concept if officially known as **LIFO** (Last-in, First-out)

Think of it exactly like a stack of plates at a buffet. You do not grab a plate from the very bottom; you grab the one right on top. When the dishwasher brings out more clean plates, they add them to the top of the pile.

    (Note: You will also occasionally hear developers talk about their "Tech Stack." In that context, they just mean the pile of different technologies they stacked together to build an app, like Python + Django + PostgreSQL).
