# Syntax

In the context of programming languages and natural languages (human languages), **_syntax_** refers to the strict structural order of the sentence and correct spelling.

It dictates exactly how you must arrange symbols and words for Python to recognize your code as valid.

***

Think of it as the structural and spelling rules for a language. 

The sentence "the a meow is a cat sound makes" has a structural order error, which is a syntax error.

***

The words exist, and their spelling is correct, which is already a good chunk of syntax; however, the structural order is incorrect. Because of this, Python would throw a syntax error and would not process the sentence.

To avoid errors, syntax (the structural order of the sentence and spelling of the words) must be correct:

"A meow is the sound a cat makes."

Ultimately, remember that **syntax refers to the structural rules of the sentence and correct spelling of the words**.
